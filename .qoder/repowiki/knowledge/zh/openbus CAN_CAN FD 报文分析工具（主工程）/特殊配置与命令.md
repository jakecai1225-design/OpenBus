构建需指定 Qt 前缀路径与编译器：`cmake -B build -S . -G "MinGW Makefiles" -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=<Qt6>/mingw_64 -DCMAKE_CXX_COMPILER=... -DCMAKE_C_COMPILER=...`，然后 `cmake --build build`；Windows 部署执行 `<Qt6>/bin/windeployqt.exe build/bin/openbus.exe`；也可用内置脚本 `python scripts/build.py all`。驱动包打包/安装/卸载通过 `python scripts/driver_tool.py pack|install|uninstall|validate`，本地市场生成通过 `python scripts/make_market.py`，插件打包通过 `python scripts/plugin_tool.py pack <目录>`。

Windows MinGW 构建需禁用预定义宏（`CMAKE_MOC_PREDEFS_CMD=""`）并使用默认 `ld.bfd` 链接器；Dev 档 `-O1 -g1` 输出至独立 `build-dev/` 目录。构建流程：`cmake -S . -B build` → `cmake --build build`。市场索引生成：`python scripts/make_market.py`；驱动包生命周期：`python scripts/driver_tool.py pack|install|uninstall|validate`；Python 插件打包：`python scripts/plugin_tool.py pack <dir>`；E2E 测试：`scripts/build_e2e.ps1` 或 `scripts/e2e_*` 源编译。

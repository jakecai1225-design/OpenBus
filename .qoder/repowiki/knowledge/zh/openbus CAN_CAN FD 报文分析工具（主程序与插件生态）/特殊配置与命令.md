构建需指定 Qt6 路径与编译器：`cmake -B build -S . -G "MinGW Makefiles" -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=".../Qt/6.8.3/mingw_64" -DCMAKE_CXX_COMPILER=".../g++.exe" -DCMAKE_C_COMPILER=".../gcc.exe"`，然后 `cmake --build build`；Windows 下用 `windeployqt.exe` 部署；也可运行 `python scripts/build.py all`；驱动包生命周期通过 `python scripts/driver_tool.py pack|install|uninstall|validate`；本地市场索引通过 `python scripts/make_market.py` 生成；插件打包通过 `python scripts/plugin_tool.py pack <目录>`。

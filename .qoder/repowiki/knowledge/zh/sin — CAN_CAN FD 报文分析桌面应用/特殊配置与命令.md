推荐使用 Python 构建脚本：`python scripts/build.py configure --build-type Release` → `python scripts/build.py build -j8` → `python scripts/build.py deploy`（自动调用 windeployqt 并补拷 Qt6PrintSupport.dll）→ `python scripts/build.py run`。也可手动 cmake 配置后 `cmake --build build`，Windows 下需 `windeployqt.exe build/bin/sin.exe` 部署运行时。默认 MinGW 路径来自环境变量 `SIN_MINGW_DIR`，Ninja 若存在于 `tools/ninja/ninja.exe` 则优先使用 Ninja 生成器。
